# Changelog

The package version in `pyproject.toml` and `sim/__init__.py` stays **0.1.0** until a tag. v0.2.0 is the structural work on `main`. It is not tagged. Tag it after a physical calibration Joshua accepts (`docs/VALIDATION.md`). The default build, with no `StabilityPolicy`, is unchanged.

## [0.2.0] — Unreleased

Do not tag this until the printed-cantilever log in `docs/VALIDATION.md` has a round whose diagnosis Joshua has signed, and the stiffness used in a released sag number is that fit. The results table is empty on purpose.

### Added

- Opt-in on-lattice inchworm (`sim/inchworm.py`). Both feet grip voxel faces, one foot steps, and a voxel is placed on a face reachable from that stance. The default geometry is unchanged: the robot still stands in an empty cell. Stride, span, place reach, and which faces are walkable are configuration. The module docstring separates those from the BILL-E and SOLL-E facts that were checked against the papers.
- Robot point loads. `estimate_sag(..., point_loads=)` adds a downward force at a voxel center. A tip load `P` on a shear chain of `n` free voxels adds `P·n/k_shear` at the tip. `split_foot_loads` divides `(robot_mass + carried_mass)·g` equally across the feet. A crane snapshot has no feet, so it still adds nothing. `StabilityPolicy.max_sag` refuses an on-lattice step whose loaded sag exceeds the cap. The default for that field is off.
- On-lattice campaign, 108 trials (`python main.py onlattice`, `docs/on_lattice.csv`). Gate off, a ratio-1 robot on the 3-voxel arm peaks at 7 against a voxels-only 6 (n = 3, no spread). The bridge inchworm is rejected, 3/3, by both the cantilever gate and the sag cap, because its only route is a one-sided deck. The crane under the cantilever gate places the far cap earlier and the peak falls from 6 to 3. Ratios 1, 3, and 10 are a sweep. SOLL-E's published ratio is stated in the summary and is not a row. `k = 1`.
- Replay draws inchworm feet on the faces named in the log, and colors include robot loads when the sample has them. Feet follow the face the viewer drew, including when the exaggerate slider moves or shrinks that voxel. A crane log is drawn as before.
- Optional structural stability gate (`sim/stability.py`), off unless a caller passes a `StabilityPolicy`. `is_stable` requires every voxel to face-connect to a column on the base, and it limits horizontal overhang. A refusal is the existing kickback. Deeds are not supports.
- Small-displacement sag estimate: voxels as nodes, one lumped axial/shear spring per face, `Ku = mg`. Closed forms for a shear-chain cantilever and an axial tower (`shear_cantilever_tip_sag`, `axial_tower_compression`, `path_chain_deflection_at`). Not a gate, and not inside the search.
- Opt-in strain log (`StructureLog` on a `RunLog`) and replay coloring: displacement or joint strain, worst-sag marker. Logs without samples play as before. Colors are the model. Default `k = 1` is not a fit. The viewer draws as-built cells unless the exaggerate slider is on. That slider sets the longest arrow as a fraction of the voxel pitch (mild value 0.20, cap 0.40) and labels the picture exaggerated and normalized.
- Gate campaign, 312 trials (`python main.py stability`, `docs/stability_cost.csv`). On the span-5 bridge, `max_cantilever` 2 cuts peak intermediate sag from a median of 15 (range 10–15, n = 6) to 6, and pays makespan and kickbacks. Stiffness in that file is `k = 1`.
- Optional cuboctahedral solid on the cubic grid (`sim/cuboct.py`). Same six face-neighbors and the same lumped springs, so the closed forms still hold. Different vertices and square-face joint sites. Cubic stays the default. Not a mechanical model of ARMADAS hardware.
- Finished-structure stability precheck (`necessary_stability_precheck`). Overhang in the finished set lower-bounds every prefix. The hollow 3×3×2 roof at cantilever limit 0 is a proved rejection (6/6, median 0.0965 ms) instead of an undecided 200-node search.
- Physical validation plan and empty log: `docs/VALIDATION.md`. One-piece PLA cantilevers, n = 3 and n = 6 free bays. Predicted self-weight tip ratio 3.5 (shear chain) against 16 (Euler–Bernoulli `n⁴`). No measured rows.
- Headless viewer capture: `scripts/render_demo.py` (Playwright + ffmpeg). About 60 seconds, two-robot bridge with displacement coloring and the span drawn in place, then a cuboct block, with title cards. The mp4 is a generated artifact, not a committed blob.
- Temporary scaffold (`sim/scaffold.py`). When the finished blueprint passes the cantilever and sag caps and no reachable order does, the planner adds the smallest set of support cells in the column under the blueprint, builds with them, and removes them in an order that stays inside the same caps. Scaffold is occupancy `SCAFFOLD`. It is solid, so a robot can walk on it, and it is not a blueprint cell. A finished structure that already fails the caps is not rescued. The secondary key after the count is `sequence_cost`, a single-robot depot-round-trip score. It is not the swarm tick count: the score charges a final return the swarm does not wait for.
- Scaffold campaign, 90 trials (`python main.py scaffold`, `docs/scaffold_campaign.csv`). Spans 4–8, crane and on-lattice inchworm, robot/voxel ratios near 1, 1/3, and 1/10 (`n_robots = max(1, round(n_voxels / divisor))` for divisor 1, 3, 10), seeds {0, 1, 2}. 88 of 90 finished with the blueprint complete and no scaffold left. The crane needed 0 scaffold cells. The inchworm needed 3 on span 4 and 2 on spans 5–8. Two seeds hit the 4000-tick cap (span 6 with 9 robots, seed 2; span 8 with 4 robots, seed 2). Caps are the finished bridge's overhang and its `worst_sag` at `k = mass = g = 1` (units of mass·g/k; stiffness not fit).
- Replay draws scaffold in construction yellow (`#f5c518`) in every color mode, including displacement. The box stays 0.96, the same size as a blueprint voxel. The run-log legend includes `SCAFFOLD`. Stills: `docs/scaffold_standing.png`, `docs/scaffold_removed.png`, `docs/scaffold_displacement.png`.
- ARMADAS shelter benchmark (`python main.py armadas`, `sim/armadas.py`). Three roles (cargo, crane, fastening). Each new face joint is its own fasten task. One feed station serves one cargo robot at a time. The stand-in shed is 256 cells with a five-voxel seed and a roof eave. It is not the Fig. 5 coordinate list. The gate stays on at `k = mass = g = 1` (units of mass·g/k, stiffness not fit). Step time is Park et al. 2023's "on the order of 20 seconds," not a Gregg 2024 duration. Load, handoff, place, and fasten are config at that same 20 s and were not fitted to the clock. One feed and one robot per role finishes in 42.972 h, which is 0.428 times the manuscript's approximately 100.4 h (also printed as 4.2 days = 100.8 h). The sweep of 1–4 feeds by 1–6 robots per role is `docs/armadas_sweep.csv` and `docs/armadas_sweep.png`. On this shed the feed station stays under 5% utilization. The binding constraint moves from the cargo robot (1–2 per role) to the dependency chain (3–6). Still: `docs/armadas_shelter.png`.

### Changed

- `python main.py stability` counts a finished structure past the cantilever limit as infeasible from the precheck. The campaign in `docs/stability_cost.csv` was re-run: 202 feasible, 110 infeasible, 0 undecided. Build metrics on the trials that still run are the same comparison the gate-cost write-up reports.
- Replay viewer draws cuboctahedra when `meta.voxel_shape` is `cuboctahedral`, and exposes `window.assemblerReplay` for the capture script.
- Replay deformation no longer multiplies the raw `mg/k` vector. Default is the as-built geometry. The slider is a fraction of the voxel pitch, capped at 0.40, and the view is labeled exaggerated and normalized. Robots are drawn on the solid under them.
- A time-expanded plan memoizes footing, neighbor lists, and stride footprints for that plan only. Successors are unchanged. Reservation checks stay live. Long-stride multi-robot walks were rebuilding the same base-step search at every tick of the horizon.

### Tests

209 tests (`python -m unittest`): traps, 3D repair, fuzz, examples, the structural screen, the precheck, strain logging, the bridge blueprint against its finished log, the stability-gate smoke run, the cuboctahedral geometry, the inchworm gait, the tip point-load closed form, a smoke run of the on-lattice campaign, scaffold insertion and removal (previously infeasible inchworm bridges become feasible, removal stays inside the caps, no scaffold when the plain plan already works), and the shelter benchmark (published figures, the sag-gated order, one fasten task per joint, the feed queue, and the committed sweep).
