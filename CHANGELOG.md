# Changelog

The package version in `pyproject.toml` and `sim/__init__.py` stays **0.1.0** until a tag. v0.2.0 is the structural work on `main`. It is not tagged. Tag it after a physical calibration Joshua accepts (`docs/VALIDATION.md`). The default build, with no `StabilityPolicy`, is unchanged.

## [0.2.0] — Unreleased

Do not tag this until the printed-cantilever log in `docs/VALIDATION.md` has a round whose diagnosis Joshua has signed, and the stiffness used in a released sag number is that fit. The results table is empty on purpose.

### Added

- Opt-in on-lattice inchworm (`sim/inchworm.py`). Both feet grip voxel faces, one foot steps, and a voxel is placed on a face reachable from that stance. The default geometry is unchanged: the robot still stands in an empty cell. Stride, span, place reach, and which faces are walkable are configuration. The module docstring separates those from the BILL-E and SOLL-E facts that were checked against the papers.
- Robot point loads. `estimate_sag(..., point_loads=)` adds a downward force at a voxel center. A tip load `P` on a shear chain of `n` free voxels adds `P·n/k_shear` at the tip. `split_foot_loads` divides `(robot_mass + carried_mass)·g` equally across the feet. A crane snapshot has no feet, so it still adds nothing. `StabilityPolicy.max_sag` refuses an on-lattice step whose loaded sag exceeds the cap. The default for that field is off.
- On-lattice campaign, 108 trials (`python main.py onlattice`, `docs/on_lattice.csv`). Gate off, a ratio-1 robot on the 3-voxel arm peaks at 7 against a voxels-only 6 (n = 3, no spread). The bridge inchworm is rejected, 3/3, by both the cantilever gate and the sag cap, because its only route is a one-sided deck. The crane under the cantilever gate places the far cap earlier and the peak falls from 6 to 3. Ratios 1, 3, and 10 are a sweep. SOLL-E's published ratio is stated in the summary and is not a row. `k = 1`.
- Replay draws inchworm feet on the faces named in the log, and colors include robot loads when the sample has them. Feet follow the face the viewer drew, including when the exaggerate slider moves or shrinks that voxel. A crane log is drawn as before.
- Optional structural stability gate (`sim/stability.py`), off unless a caller passes a `StabilityPolicy`. `is_stable` requires every voxel to face-connect to a column on the base, and it limits horizontal overhang. A refusal is the existing kickback. Deeds are not supports.
- Small-displacement sag estimate: voxels as nodes, one lumped axial/shear spring per face, `Ku = mg`. Closed forms for a shear-chain cantilever and an axial tower (`shear_cantilever_tip_sag`, `axial_tower_compression`, `path_chain_deflection_at`). Not a gate, and not inside the search.
- Opt-in strain log (`StructureLog` on a `RunLog`) and replay coloring: displacement or joint strain, worst-sag marker. Logs without samples play as before. Colors are the model. Default `k = 1` is not a fit. The viewer draws as-built cells unless the exaggerate slider is on. That slider sets the longest arrow as a fraction of the voxel pitch (mild value 0.20, cap 0.40) and labels the picture exaggerated and normalized.
- Gate campaign, 312 trials (`python main.py stability`, `docs/stability_cost.csv`). On the span-5 bridge, `max_cantilever` 2 cuts peak intermediate sag from a median of 15 (range 10–15, n = 6) to 6, and pays makespan and kickbacks. Stiffness in that file is `k = 1`.
- Optional cuboctahedral solid on the cubic grid (`sim/cuboct.py`). Same six face-neighbors and the same lumped springs, so the closed forms still hold. Different vertices and square-face joint sites. Cubic stays the default. Not a mechanical model of ARMADAS hardware.
- Finished-structure stability precheck (`necessary_stability_precheck`). Overhang in the finished set lower-bounds every prefix. The hollow 3×3×2 roof at cantilever limit 0 is a proved rejection (6/6, median 0.0965 ms) instead of an undecided 200-node search.
- Physical validation plan and empty log: `docs/VALIDATION.md`. One-piece PLA cantilevers, n = 3 and n = 6 free bays. Predicted self-weight tip ratio 3.5 (shear chain) against 16 (Euler–Bernoulli `n⁴`). No measured rows.
- Headless viewer capture: `scripts/render_demo.py` (Playwright + ffmpeg). About 60 seconds, two-robot bridge with displacement coloring and the span drawn in place, then a cuboct block, with title cards. The mp4 is a generated artifact, not a committed blob.
- Temporary scaffold (`sim/scaffold.py`). When the finished blueprint passes the cantilever and sag caps and no reachable order does, the planner adds the smallest set of support cells in the column under the blueprint, builds with them, and removes them in an order that stays inside the same caps. Scaffold is occupancy `SCAFFOLD`. It is solid, so a robot can walk on it, and it is not a blueprint cell. A finished structure that already fails the caps is not rescued. The secondary key after the count is `sequence_cost`, a single-robot depot-round-trip score. It is not the swarm tick count: the score charges a final return the swarm does not wait for.
- Scaffold campaign, 90 trials (`python main.py scaffold`, `docs/scaffold_campaign.csv`). Spans 4–8, crane and on-lattice inchworm, robot/voxel ratios near 1, 1/3, and 1/10 (`n_robots = max(1, round(n_voxels / divisor))` for divisor 1, 3, 10), seeds {0, 1, 2}. 88 of 90 finished with the blueprint complete and no scaffold left. The crane needed 0 scaffold cells. The inchworm needed 3 on span 4 and 2 on spans 5–8. Two seeds hit the 4000-tick cap (span 6 with 9 robots, seed 2; span 8 with 4 robots, seed 2). Caps are the finished bridge's overhang and its `worst_sag` at `k = mass = g = 1` (units of mass·g/k; stiffness not fit).
- Replay draws scaffold in construction yellow (`#f5c518`) in every color mode, including displacement. The box stays 0.96, the same size as a blueprint voxel. The run-log legend includes `SCAFFOLD`. Stills: `docs/scaffold_standing.png`, `docs/scaffold_removed.png`, `docs/scaffold_displacement.png`.
- Face-joint strength beside stiffness (`JointStrength`: axial compression, axial tension, shear, and an optional moment). `joint_loads` reads the force from the existing spring-network solve, self-weight plus any robot point loads. At `k = mass = g = 1` that force is `mass·g`. A moment is stored and is not produced by the solve. `joints_within` fails a joint whose demand/capacity exceeds `safety_factor` (default 1, range `(0, 1]`). The planner, the choreographer, and `plan_with_scaffold` take an optional `JointCap` and reject a prefix that fails it. A finished blueprint that already fails is not rescued by more scaffold.
- ARMADAS 3×3×3 compression, checked against the Science Robotics HTML (doi:10.1126/scirobotics.adi2746) and NTRS 20230005194, which do not print the same strength. Published HTML: 11.38 kPa (SD ±0.51 kPa), failure load more than 9000 N (a lower bound), stiffness 1.1129 MPa. Manuscript: 11.17 kPa (±0.77 kPa), stiffness SD ±0.0430 MPa. The HTML stiffness SD ("±0.779 kPa" on a MPa value) is unconfirmed and is not used. Even-share face compression from the published strength and the 3×3 bounding box: 1057.2365952 N (SD 47.3805504 N from the strength SD only). Implied mean load 9515.1293568 N is derived, not printed. Fastener-pair tension 797.3 N (±8.71 N) is a fixture result and is not the face capacity. Shear and moment were not measured. `armadas_joint_strength` returns the compression only. It is not applied to a `k = 1` solve.
- Joint-limit experiment (`python main.py joints`, `docs/joint_limits_*.csv`, `docs/joint_limits.png`). Clamp cantilevers are shear-limited, clamp towers are compression-limited, and bridges and the 256-cell shelter are limited by compression, shear, and sag together. A joint cap at the finished demand does not change the scaffold count (crane spans 4–6 stay at 0; inchworm span 4 stays at 3). Half that demand makes the blueprint infeasible. The count does not step up.
- Replay colors joint utilization (demand/capacity) when the log has a strength. Scaffold stays `#f5c518`. Stills at deform 0: `docs/joint_cantilever_shear.png`, `docs/joint_cantilever_utilization.png`, `docs/joint_tower_axial.png`, `docs/joint_tower_utilization.png`, `docs/joint_bridge_utilization.png`, `docs/joint_bridge_onesided_utilization.png`, `docs/joint_shelter_utilization.png`.
- Placement error and a self-aligning capture (`sim/precision.py`, `python main.py precision`). Each new voxel draws a position and one angle. The default is uniform inside the ARMADAS requirement box, ±1.78 cm and ±3° (Gregg et al., Science Robotics 2024, doi:10.1126/scirobotics.adi2746, NTRS 20230005194). That box is a requirement, not a measured histogram. A Gaussian with the same variance (`half / sqrt(3)`) is available; that sigma is not measured. Skis pass ±1.78 cm in-plane. Claws and grippers pass 1.34 cm out-of-plane. The angle gate is the same ±3°. A draw inside every mating envelope snaps to the elastic average of those faces. The snap is exact: the printed <0.1 mm (body) and <0.2 mm (Fig. 4 caption) residuals are not sampled. A miss sets the existing `DEFECT` flag, so inspect / remove / replace already covers it. `precision=None` leaves the Bernoulli stream unchanged. Scaffold does not draw. `k` is not an input. Sag elsewhere stays the unfitted `k = 1`.
- Precision campaign (`docs/precision_campaign.csv`, `docs/precision_summary.txt`, `docs/precision_rms.png`, `docs/precision_fail.png`, `docs/precision_envelope.png`, `docs/precision_cantilever.png`). Cantilevers of 3, 6, 12, and 24 free cells, bridges of pier gap 4, 6, 8, 12, and 16 (n = 40), and the 256-cell shelter (n = 16), seeds fixed, up to 40 attempts. Snap seats every trial. Position RMS on those rows is numerical zero (at most 1.00637×10⁻¹⁵ m). The pooled miss rate sits on the closed form: 0.247191 on a single-mate chain, 0.254–0.282 on the bridges, 0.445993 against 0.431871 on the shelter. Open-loop tip medians grow from 0.0284 m at 3 free cells to 0.405 m at 24, and from 0.0424 m at bridge gap 4 to 0.268 m at gap 16. Gate-without-snap finishes every cantilever and 11/40, 3/40, 1/40, 0/40, 0/40 of the bridges at those gaps, and 0/16 shelters. Halving the ski and claw takes the miss rate to about 0.91. Doubling them takes it to 0. The IEEE Aerospace 2024 inch RMS figures (doi:10.1109/aero58975.2024.10521407, NTRS 20240000666) are a strut-length study and are not rows. The still is true scale, seed index 30, transverse y.
- Seeded fault injection (`sim/faults.py`, `python main.py faults`). Six channels, each a configured probability: a capture miss the precision model rejects (a scripted 5 cm draw, outside the 1.34 cm claw), a dropped voxel, a mid-task stall, a robot failure, a failed fastener on a face the joint model already names, and a feed-station outage. A rate of zero does not draw. `faults=None` leaves the swarm and the shelter schedule unchanged. Recovery is the existing path: re-place through inspect/remove, re-route, reassign, and scaffold only when resequence has no order. The stability, sag, and joint gates stay on. A fault that starts while another recovery is open is a cascade, logged at rate 0. That count is not a second configured probability. `k` is not an input.
- Fault campaign, 36 trials (`docs/fault_campaign.csv`, `docs/fault_events.csv`, `docs/fault_summary.txt`, `docs/fault_completion.png`, `docs/fault_overhead.png`, `docs/fault_cascades.png`, `docs/fault_recovery.png`). Span-5 bridge, length-3 cantilever, 256-cell shelter, seeds {0, 1, 2}, one probability on every channel in {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20}. Those probabilities are configuration, not a measurement. Completion 36/36. Bridge overhead at 0.20 is median 1.659 (1.171–1.829, n=3) against a rate-0 median of 82 ticks. Cantilever overhead at 0.20 is median 4.375 (0.1667–6, n=3) against 24 ticks. Shelter overhead at 0.20 is median 0.5051 (0.4447–0.5602, n=3) against 111860 s (31.072 h with two feeds and two robots per role, not the 1×1 baseline of 42.972 h). Shelter cascades at that rate are median 605 (588–656) against median 1012 faults. Scaffold insertions are 0; the crane order did not go empty. Still: `docs/fault_recovery_still.png`, deform 0, red defect at `(2, 2, 3)`, 0.96 boxes with a gap. `k = 1`.
- ARMADAS shelter benchmark (`python main.py armadas`, `sim/armadas.py`). Three roles (cargo, crane, fastening). Each new face joint is its own fasten task. One feed station serves one cargo robot at a time. The stand-in shed is 256 cells with a five-voxel seed and a roof eave. It is not the Fig. 5 coordinate list. The gate stays on at `k = mass = g = 1` (units of mass·g/k, stiffness not fit). Step time is Park et al. 2023's "on the order of 20 seconds," not a Gregg 2024 duration. Load, handoff, place, and fasten are config at that same 20 s and were not fitted to the clock. One feed and one robot per role finishes in 42.972 h, which is 0.428 times the manuscript's approximately 100.4 h (also printed as 4.2 days = 100.8 h). The sweep of 1–4 feeds by 1–6 robots per role is `docs/armadas_sweep.csv` and `docs/armadas_sweep.png`. On this shed the feed station stays under 5% utilization. The binding constraint moves from the cargo robot (1–2 per role) to the dependency chain (3–6). Still: `docs/armadas_shelter.png`.
- Versioned interchange (`schemas/discrete-assembly-1.0.0.json`, `sim/format.py`). Format name `discrete-assembly`, version `1.0.0`. Four kinds: blueprint (lattice, pitch, cells, seed, supports, spring model), robots (cell motion or inchworm gait, roles, fault rates), event log (placements, removals, faults, recoveries, gate verdicts), and a scenario that binds them. The checker is a draft 2020-12 subset, so the dependency list stays NumPy and Matplotlib. `k = 1` is not a fit: an unfitted note must say so, and a fitted model does not report `mass*g/k`. `python -m sim.scenarios` writes the existing geometries into `benchmarks/tasks/`.
- Public benchmark (`python main.py bench`, `benchmarks/`). Twenty-one tasks: bridges of span 4–8, cantilevers, the closed-form arm and tower, the 256-cell shelter, crane and inchworm scaffold cases, and two seeded fault tasks. The table reports completion, makespan, peak sag, joint utilization, scaffold count, and fault recovery. Stock run, `k = mass = g = 1`: shelter 42.972 h, peak sag 48.7104, joint utilization 2.78677; inchworm span 4 inserts 3 scaffold cells; crane spans 4 and 8 insert 0; cantilever arm and tower peak at 6; fault wall 7 faults and 7 recoveries; fault tower one `re-place`. Docs: `docs/FORMAT.md`, `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`.

### Fixed

- Bridge spans 7 and 8 no longer sit at the 8000-tick cap. The stepper starts beside the far pier. Nearest claim grew that cantilever to the finished limit, then reclaimed the nearer floating deck cells: cooldown 8 expired before the scan reached the other pier (completion 0.5 and 0.454545, 1276 and 1275 gate refusals). A cell the structural gate refuses is deferred until a placement or a resequence changes the prefix. The caps are the finished structure's own: span 7 overhang 3 and sag 10, span 8 overhang 4 and sag 12.5. Both rows now complete, in 130 and 150 ticks, peak sag 10 and 12.5, scaffold 0. `k = 1` is not a fit.

### Changed

- `python main.py stability` counts a finished structure past the cantilever limit as infeasible from the precheck. The campaign in `docs/stability_cost.csv` was re-run: 202 feasible, 110 infeasible, 0 undecided. Build metrics on the trials that still run are the same comparison the gate-cost write-up reports.
- Replay viewer draws cuboctahedra when `meta.voxel_shape` is `cuboctahedral`, and exposes `window.assemblerReplay` for the capture script.
- Replay deformation no longer multiplies the raw `mg/k` vector. Default is the as-built geometry. The slider is a fraction of the voxel pitch, capped at 0.40, and the view is labeled exaggerated and normalized. Robots are drawn on the solid under them.
- A time-expanded plan memoizes footing, neighbor lists, and stride footprints for that plan only. Successors are unchanged. Reservation checks stay live. Long-stride multi-robot walks were rebuilding the same base-step search at every tick of the horizon.
