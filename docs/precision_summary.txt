Reading. With snap on, a seated voxel is back on the ideal center, so RMS does not grow with length. The miss rate is the placement box against the claw, and a second mating axis (the bridge's far cap, the shelter's corners) makes it stricter. Without the check, the ±3° walk moves the tip. Without the snap but with the check, a chain still finishes and a long bridge does not: the far pier is outside the envelope of a deck that has already wandered. k is not used.
